Frequently asked questions

Is ORION only for designers?
No. ORION is for product teams, engineers, marketers, and anyone who needs to bring ideas to life faster. The AI adapts to your discipline.
How much will it cost?
We're still finalizing pricing. Early waitlist members will receive significant lifetime discounts and priority access.
Can I use it offline?
Yes. The core canvas, assets, and local AI models will continue to work without internet. Cloud features require connection.
When is the exact launch date?
We are targeting July 2025. The exact date will be announced to the waitlist first.
